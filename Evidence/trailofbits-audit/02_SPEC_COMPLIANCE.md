# FilsPay: specification-to-code review

Baseline/date/method: see `01_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Requirements are drawn from repository documents, not presumed authoritative merely because an ADR says Accepted. Contradictory documents are reported explicitly. Source-level divergences are not automatically vulnerabilities or deployed defects.

Verdicts: implemented, partial, contradicted, absent, stronger-than-spec, undecidable. Confidence below is confidence in the cited comparison, not in deployment state.

## Reverse-direction behavior not safely inferred from documents

- API keys intentionally carry merchant-wide authority despite no role field: `requireMerchantAuth.ts:137-156`.
- Public checkout returns an active key hash: `paymentLinks/read.ts:43-52,73-74`. Not equivalent to returning the raw bearer key.
- `savedFilters.ts:44-46` claims a 50-row cap in the wrong migration; the actual table is `0069_reporting_saved_filters.sql`. No independent security impact assigned.
- `/v1/reporting/export-summary` has no auth prehandler yet dereferences `request.merchantAuth`: `routes/reporting.ts:79-85`. The supported conclusion is unauthenticated failure, not successful disclosure.
- Explicit separation of admin-cookie authentication from merchant-cookie authentication is stronger than a naive shared-cookie model: `adminAuth/middleware.ts:24-40`; `requireMerchantAuth.ts:46-101`.

## Refutation and uncertainty checks

The lead investigator reread the highest-impact comparisons, including ADR integer rules, the view/session predicate, cancellation chain, impersonation gates, callback validation, CSV parser/posting, payout effects and rebuild source. Independent reviewers challenged tenant and rebate candidates. The installed plugin's full slash-command fan-out/structured-record implementation was not available through this session's tools; this report does not claim its automated workflow ran.

Authoritative document ownership, actual deployed schema, acquirer rounding rules, bank integration, singleton workers, externally scheduled jobs and operational signoff remain unverified. Proposed/paused settlement-v2 documents are not treated as shipped requirements. Snapshot exports can corroborate arithmetic but do not establish current balances or losses.
